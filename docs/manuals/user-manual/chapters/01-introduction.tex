\section{Purpose of this Manual}
This user manual provides comprehensive instructions for setting up, operating, and maintaining the Mountain Climber Health \& GPS Tracker system. It is designed to guide both climbers and base camp operators through the safe and effective use of the equipment during mountaineering expeditions. 

\section{Intended Audience}
This document is primarily intended for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mountaineers and Climbers:} Individuals wearing the tracking devices and utilizing the mobile application for health monitoring and SOS alerts.
    \item \textbf{Base Camp Operators:} Personnel responsible for monitoring the dashboard, tracking climber positions, and coordinating emergency responses.
    \item \textbf{Expedition Leaders:} Managing equipment logistics, device registration, and firmware updates via the web portal.
\end{itemize}

\section{Safety Warnings}
Mountaineering is an inherently dangerous activity. The Mountain Climber Health \& GPS Tracker is designed to \textit{assist} in safety and monitoring; it does not guarantee survival or rescue in extreme conditions.

\begin{cautionbox}
\textbf{Harsh Weather and Electronic Equipment:} 
While the devices are designed for rugged outdoor use, extreme temperatures (below -20$^\circ$C or above +50$^\circ$C) can significantly degrade battery life and affect sensor accuracy. Always keep devices properly secured and shielded from direct exposure to severe blizzards or prolonged submersion in water.
\end{cautionbox}

\begin{warningbox}
\textbf{Reliance on Technology:} Do not rely solely on this system for your safety. Always carry traditional navigation tools (map and compass), standard communication equipment, and comprehensive first-aid supplies.
\end{warningbox}

\section{System Overview in Brief}
The Mountain Climber Health \& GPS Tracker is an advanced IoT-based safety system. It continuously monitors a climber's vital signs (heart rate and SpO2) and GPS location, transmitting this critical data back to a base camp via a long-range LoRa mesh network. In the event of an emergency, climbers can trigger SOS alerts. The system also supports two-way text messaging and provides a comprehensive dashboard for real-time monitoring by base camp operators, ensuring rapid response to any emerging health or safety concerns.
