\chapter{Troubleshooting}
\label{ch:troubleshooting}

This chapter provides solutions for common issues that may arise during the operation of the Mountain Climber Health \& GPS Tracker system. If a problem persists after following these troubleshooting steps, please consult the maintenance guidelines or contact technical support.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Common Troubleshooting Issues}
\label{tab:troubleshooting}
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.25\linewidth}p{0.35\linewidth}p{0.4\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Problem} & \textbf{Possible Cause} & \textbf{Solution} \\ \midrule

% BLE Issues
\textbf{Mobile app fails to connect to Climber Device via BLE} & Bluetooth is disabled on the phone. & Enable Bluetooth in the phone settings. \\
 & Device is out of range. & Keep the phone within 5 meters of the Climber Main Device. \\
 & Another device is currently connected. & Disconnect any previously paired devices and restart the pairing process. \\

% GPS Issues
\textbf{GPS module not getting a location fix} & No clear view of the sky. & Move to an open outdoor area away from tall buildings, dense trees, or deep valleys. \\
 & Cold start initialization taking too long. & Wait up to 5-10 minutes if the device has been powered off for a long time. \\
 & Antenna connector is loose. & Check and securely fasten the ceramic GPS antenna connection. \\

% LoRa Issues
\textbf{LoRa communication out of range or dropping packets} & Physical obstructions blocking the signal. & Move to a higher elevation or deploy a Repeater node. \\
 & Antenna is damaged or improperly oriented. & Ensure the antenna is pointing vertically and is not bent or broken. \\
 & Frequency or parameters mismatch. & Verify that all devices are configured to 433MHz, SF7, BW 125kHz via firmware settings. \\

% Dashboard Issues
\textbf{Dashboard not receiving data from Base Camp Unit} & USB Serial connection is loose or disconnected. & Reconnect the USB cable between the Base Camp Unit and the laptop. \\
 & Incorrect COM port selected in dashboard. & Check Device Manager (Windows) or \texttt{/dev/tty*} (Mac/Linux) and select the correct COM port. \\
 & Base Camp Unit not powered on. & Verify that the ESP32 Base Camp unit is powered and the power LED is on. \\

% Sensor Issues
\textbf{Wristband not reading Heart Rate or SpO2} & Sensor window is dirty or obstructed. & Clean the MAX30102 sensor window with a soft, dry cloth. \\
 & Wristband worn too loosely or over clothing. & Fasten the wristband snugly against the skin on the wrist. \\
 & Excessive arm movement during reading. & Keep the arm still for 10-15 seconds to allow the sensor to acquire a stable reading. \\

% SOS Issues
\textbf{SOS button not triggering emergency alert} & Button not held for the required duration. & Press and hold the physical SOS button for at least 3 seconds. \\
 & Device battery is completely depleted. & Charge or replace the 18650 battery immediately. \\

% App Issues
\textbf{Mobile application crashes or freezes} & Outdated app version or OS incompatibility. & Update the mobile app to the latest version via the Portal. Restart the smartphone. \\
 & Background permissions denied. & Ensure the app has permissions for Location, Bluetooth, and Background Execution. \\

% Serial Port Issues
\textbf{Serial port not detected by laptop} & Missing CP2102/CH340 USB-to-Serial drivers. & Download and install the appropriate driver for your operating system. \\
 & Using a power-only USB cable. & Replace with a high-quality data-sync micro-USB cable. \\

% Battery Issues
\textbf{Battery draining excessively fast} & Transmission interval set too high. & Increase the delay between data transmissions via the app settings. \\
 & Operating in extreme cold temperatures. & Keep the device insulated; cold temperatures severely reduce Li-ion battery capacity. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}
