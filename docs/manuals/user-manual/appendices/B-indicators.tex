\chapter{LED \& Buzzer Indicator Reference}
\label{app:indicators}

This appendix provides a comprehensive reference guide for all visual (LED) and auditory (buzzer) feedback signals generated by the system devices. Understanding these indicators is crucial for rapid assessment of the system's operational state in the field.

\section{LED Visual Indicators}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{LED Status Indicators}
\label{tab:led-indicators}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{LED Color / Pattern} & \textbf{Component} & \textbf{System State / Meaning} \\ \midrule
\textbf{Solid Green} & Main Device / Wristband & Device powered on, functioning normally. \\
\textbf{Blinking Blue (Slow)} & Wristband & BLE advertising mode; waiting for connection. \\
\textbf{Solid Blue} & Wristband & BLE successfully connected to Main Device/App. \\
\textbf{Rapid Red Flashing} & Main Device & \textbf{SOS Active:} Emergency mode triggered. \\
\textbf{Blinking Green} & Base Camp Unit & Receiving LoRa data packet successfully. \\
\textbf{Blinking Yellow} & Main Device & Attempting to acquire GPS fix. \\
\textbf{Solid Yellow} & Main Device & GPS fix successfully acquired. \\
\textbf{Solid Red} & Main Device (Charger) & Battery is currently charging. \\
\textbf{Solid Green (Charger)} & Main Device (Charger) & Battery is fully charged. \\
\textbf{Slow Red Pulsing} & Main Device & Low battery warning ($< 20\%$ capacity). \\
\textbf{Alternating Red/Blue} & Main Device & OTA Firmware update in progress. Do not power off. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section{Buzzer Auditory Indicators}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Buzzer Status Patterns}
\label{tab:buzzer-indicators}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Buzzer Pattern} & \textbf{Component} & \textbf{System State / Meaning} \\ \midrule
\textbf{Single Short Beep} & Main Device & Text message or successful configuration received. \\
\textbf{Double Short Beep} & Main Device & GPS location fix successfully acquired. \\
\textbf{Continuous Loud Tone} & Main Device & \textbf{SOS Active:} Emergency mode triggered locally. \\
\textbf{Three Rapid Beeps} & Base Camp Unit & Incoming SOS alert received from a climber. \\
\textbf{Long Beep (2s)} & Main Device & System powering on or shutting down. \\
\textbf{Periodic Short Beep} & Main Device & Low battery warning (repeats every 5 minutes). \\
\textbf{Error Melody} & Main Device & Hardware failure (e.g., sensor disconnected). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{infobox}
The auditory buzzer patterns on the Climber Main Device can be muted via the mobile application settings, except for the continuous SOS emergency tone, which cannot be disabled for safety reasons.
\end{infobox}
